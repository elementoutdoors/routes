Two counter flip books, one per PDF in this folder:
  1. Bike On Ride Maps Flip Book (print ready).pdf  - 15 pages (cover + 14 rides)

FINISHED SIZE
A4 landscape, 297 x 210 mm. Printed single sided.

FILES
PDF pages are 303 x 216 mm: A4 plus 3 mm bleed on every edge. No crop marks.
Colour is RGB; please convert to CMYK and send a proof first.
All text is at least 5 mm inside the trim.

STOCK (durable, handled daily on a shop counter)
350gsm silk or satin card, MATT laminated both sides.
Matt, not gloss: customers scan QR codes on every page and gloss glare stops phones reading them.
Alternative if you have it: synthetic waterproof stock (about 250 to 300 micron polypropylene or polyester).

BINDING
Wire-o (or coil) along the TOP long edge, calendar style, so pages flip up and over.
The top 12 mm of every page is kept clear of text for the binding holes.
Clear PVC front cover and a rigid back board. An easel or stand back so it sits upright on the counter, if available.

PROOF CHECK
Please confirm on the proof that the QR codes on each page scan (three per ride page: App Store, Google Play and the ride maps).

QUANTITY
To be confirmed when quoting (suggest a quote for 1 and 2 of each book).
